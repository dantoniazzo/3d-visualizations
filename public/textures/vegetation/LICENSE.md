`leaves.png` and `grass-tuft.png` are the leaf and grass cards of
[fluffytree-threejs](https://github.com/leoawen/fluffytree-threejs), and
`ground-gradient.jpg` its ground's gradient, sand to green, all taken from
its `assets/landscape-glb.glb` — as is `public/models/fluffy-tree.glb`, its
tree, stood at the origin — whose foliage and grass shaders
`frontend/Experience/World/Vegetation/shaders.js` is adapted from.
